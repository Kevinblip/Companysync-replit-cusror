WHY
Stuck Job alerts fire because a job stays In Progress longer than stuck_job_threshold_days (default 10). Roofing jobs wait on insurance, and one property often has roof + siding + gutters as separate jobs. That is normal, not stuck. YICN got CRITICAL emails for Joyce Gerhard (11 days In Progress), LeTrice Duckworth (reminder #2), and PALMETTO WOODS. Suggested reason is always “Stalled.”

FIX
Find the stuck-job detector (search stuck_job, stuck_job_threshold_days, stuck_job_re_alert_days, stuck_job_alerts_enabled). Change detection only. Do not rewrite the whole alerts system.

1) A job is stuck ONLY if:
   - status is In Progress (or equivalent open/active), AND
   - there has been NO activity for stuck_job_threshold_days, AND
   - it is not marked Waiting.
   Activity means any of: note, photo/file, status change, estimate sent, inspection, call/SMS log, calendar event on that job.
   Clock starts from last activity, not from the day it entered In Progress.

2) Waiting reasons staff can set (and the detector can suggest): Insurance / adjuster, Materials, Permit or HOA, Customer, Other trade on same property.
   If a Waiting status or waiting_reason field does not exist, add the smallest one so staff can mark it. Do not backfill or rewrite existing YICN jobs.
   Waiting jobs never get type stuck_job CRITICAL mail and never get reminder #2.

3) Same property (same address, or same company + same house file): if another open job there had activity inside the threshold, do not flag this job stuck. Suggest Waiting — other trade.

4) Keep Sarah/assistant settings: stuck_job_alerts_enabled, stuck_job_threshold_days, stuck_job_re_alert_days. Re-alert only if it still meets the new stuck definition.

5) Suggested category: Forgotten or Stalled only when there is truly no activity. Never default a quiet-but-waiting insurance job to Stalled.
