---
name: Financial forecast evidence
description: Why collections forecasts use auditable baselines rather than unvalidated AI-generated amounts and confidence scores.
---

Prefer an auditable historical baseline over an LLM-generated financial prediction. Keep recorded cash, invoice balances due, and forecast assumptions visibly separate.

**Why:** The user challenged a forecast whose AI claimed a business downturn from a partial month and displayed unsupported confidence percentages. A plausible narrative is not measured forecasting accuracy. Sparse historical data also does not establish recurring seasonality.

**How to apply:** Future forecasting improvements should use confirmed collection timing or backtested models. Do not restore model-invented numeric confidence or infer zero sales from missing lead-conversion flags. Explain missing history and assumptions, and never present a due date or scheduled job as guaranteed cash.
