name: Insurance claim welcome scope
description: Customer introductions must target new insurance claims only and use explicitly selected public team details.
---

Send team-introduction welcome emails automatically for new insurance claims only. Do not backfill or bulk-email existing claims.

**Why:** The owner specifically chose “Automatically for new claims” rather than sending to existing claims.

**How to apply:** Preserve the new-claim boundary when expanding trigger paths. Routine edits and claim-number corrections are not new claims. A customer and their linked insurance project must not produce duplicate introductions.

Use explicitly selected staff profiles, customer-facing responsibilities, and reviewed business/customer-service phone numbers. Never infer a person’s identity from an abbreviated name or expose their personal cell as a fallback.

**Why:** The owner wants homeowners to meet their office, production, and project managers with photos and responsibilities; those are customer-facing facts, not assumptions.

**How to apply:** Keep the automation off until the company’s team introduction has been configured and reviewed.
