---
name: Photo material map authority
description: Photo wall material classification owns siding quantities and Present readiness.
---

The photo material map is the authority for wall takeoff: only `lap_siding` and
`vertical_siding` contribute to siding area; stone, brick, stucco, glass, other,
and unknown regions remain outside the siding quantity. Opening deductions must
use the siding share of the opening's own wall face.

**Why:** A single house can combine siding with brick or stone veneer, and a
house-wide siding assumption overstates siding while charging masonry openings
to the wrong material.

**How to apply:** Preserve per-face regions through every manual rebuild, expose
separate siding/stone/brick rows, and block customer-facing Present when photo
material data is missing, incomplete, or ambiguous.
