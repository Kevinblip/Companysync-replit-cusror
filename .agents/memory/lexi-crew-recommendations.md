---
name: Lexi crew recommendations
description: Rules for recommending subcontractor crews without overstating coverage or availability.
---

Lexi crew suggestions must combine the subcontractor's active status, specialty, rating, completed-job history, and listed work radius.

**Why:** A high rating alone does not mean a crew covers the job location or is free on the requested date. A stored radius also does not prove coverage unless the job-to-crew distance is known.

**How to apply:** Use the subcontractor search to rank candidates, then query the Build Schedule for the requested date and inspect crew assignments. Say “no conflict found” rather than “confirmed available” unless a real commitment exists.
