---
name: Mobile customer browsing
description: The requested mobile scrolling and card behavior for the Customers page
---

**Rule:** On phones, Customers should follow Leads' natural page scrolling and readable swipe-card layout, with key contact and inspection details visible.

**Why:** The owner has repeatedly reported that the customer list is difficult to reach and read on a phone.

**How to apply:** When changing Customers mobile UI, avoid a nested fixed-height list scroller, keep summary controls compact, and verify the list at a phone-sized viewport.
