---
name: Subcontractor trade scope
description: Custom trade categories and work-sample review before assigning subcontractors.
---

Subcontractor specialties must allow user-defined job categories, not just the
predefined trade list.

**Why:** The user specifically requested adding categories such as Decks from the
subcontractor specialty selector. The product's job scope is broader than the
initial roofing and home-exterior trade presets.

**How to apply:** Preserve custom specialty names when extending subcontractor
forms and avoid converting the specialty field into a closed preset-only enum.

## Pre-award work review

Work examples belong with each subcontractor so staff can review them before
giving that subcontractor a job. Keep the portfolio compact and compress photos
before uploading.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked to see examples of a subcontractor's work to
confirm suitability before awarding the job, and requested compressed photos.

**How to apply:** Preserve the subcontractor-bound work-sample review when
changing assignment or subcontractor screens; do not treat samples as photos of
an already assigned project.
