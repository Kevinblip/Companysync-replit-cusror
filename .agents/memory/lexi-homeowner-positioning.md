---
name: Lexi homeowner positioning
description: Product boundary between staff Lexi and the homeowner-facing assistant
---
Introduce the homeowner-facing assistant as “Lexi, your AI project update assistant,” not the company's COO. Keep the staff assistant and homeowner assistant as separate access modes even though they share the Lexi name.

**Why:** The user chose Lexi for homeowner communications but asked whether the customer assistant should be the same COO persona. Staff authority and internal knowledge must not imply homeowner access.

**How to apply:** Customer introductions must disclose that Lexi is AI. Homeowner answers are limited to explicitly customer-visible progress; missing or ambiguous identity/project information requires a human handoff rather than inferred access.
