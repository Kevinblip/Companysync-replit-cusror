1. Coverage Period
YICN Roofing (the "Contractor") warrants to the original Property Owner that the roofing system installed will be free from defects in
workmanship for a period of ten (10) years from the date of substantial completion.
2. Scope of Warranty
This warranty covers leaks or structural failures directly resulting from errors in the installation process, including:
Improper fastening of shingles or underlayment.
Incorrect installation of flashing (chimneys, valleys, pipe boots).
Failure to adhere to manufacturer-specified installation patterns.
3. Remedy
If a workmanship defect is identified during the 10-year period, the Contractor will provide the labor and materials necessary to repair the
specific affected area at no cost to the Property Owner.
4. Exclusions
This warranty specifically excludes damage caused by:
Acts of God: Hail, winds exceeding manufacturer ratings, lightning, or fallen trees.
Existing Structures: Movement or settling of the building's foundation or roof deck.
Third-Party Alterations: Modifications made by other contractors (e.g., satellite dish installation, solar panels, or gutter repairs).
Lack of Maintenance: Failure by the owner to keep the roof free of debris or ice dams.
Material Defects: Failures in the shingles or products themselves (which are covered by the Manufacturer’s Warranty).
5. Transferability
This warranty is non-transferable unless otherwise agreed upon in writing at the time of title transfer. A one-time transfer may be
permitted within the first two (2) years of the warranty period, subject to a professional inspection by the Contractor.

To report a claim, the Property Owner must notify the Contractor within 48 hours of discovering the leak or defect. Failure to provide
timely notice that results in additional interior damage may void the labor coverage for those consequential damages.
